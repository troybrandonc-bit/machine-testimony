Conformance

Check a record

Drop a Testimony Record and see which level it reaches. Nothing is uploaded. The validator runs in this page, so a record about your customers never leaves your machine.

Drop a .jsonl record here, or paste one below.

If you do not have a record yet

Any system can emit one and the format needs nothing of ours. If you use LangGraph, this takes about five minutes:

pip install testimony-langgraph

Your node names the action so the operator's risk table can classify it, and the identity of whoever approves comes from your authentication layer rather than from anything the model wrote. The adapter will not invent an approver, because there is nothing in LangGraph for it to find, so a graph resumed the ordinary way produces a record that says so plainly instead of claiming a human was involved.

The same validator ships with that package as testimony-validate, so a record can be checked at a terminal as well as here.

What the levels mean

LevelProperty
TR-1Recorded. The record is well formed and append-only.
TR-2Explained. Beliefs cite their evidence, or say there is none, and both sides of a disagreement are kept.
TR-3Gated. Consequential actions carry a verdict, and approvals name a person whose identity came from authentication.
TR-4Verifiable. The record publishes a scheme under which alteration would be detected.

The levels are cumulative, so a record can satisfy a level it has not reached. Each level's own result is reported separately for that reason.
